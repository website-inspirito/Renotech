Two-stage cooling

How much cooling can be achieved?

Follow ambient air at 42.2°C dry-bulb and 23.5°C wet-bulb through a Renotech indirect-direct unit.

01
Ambient air in

Hot outdoor air at 42.2°C is drawn in through the pre-filters by the fan.

02
Stage 1 · Indirect

The air-to-air heat exchanger cools the air sensibly, without adding moisture. At 65% efficiency it leaves at 30.0°C.

03
Stage 2 · Direct

The cooling pad then cools it adiabatically at 90% efficiency, down to 20.9°C.

04
Supply air out

Supply air leaves 2.6°C below the ambient wet-bulb temperature, at lower power than a compressor system.
